The Operational Challenge

Why Standard Face Detection Fails on Bodycams

⚡

Violent Motion Blur

Sudden officer movements cause rapid shutter smearing. Features dissolve for 5-15 frames, causing single-frame unblurred flicker leaks.

🕶️

Extreme Angles & Occlusion

Chest-level mounting creates steep upward/downward vantage points, heavy hair occlusion, and severe facial profiles.

⚖️

Fail-Closed Philosophy

Standard CV optimizes for average mAP (95% detection). In privacy, a 5% leak rate is a catastrophic compliance failure. We quarantine uncertain jobs to NEEDS_REVIEW.

Core Rule: A pipeline should NEVER output SAFE when uncertain. All anomalies are flagged for human review. Strict Fail-Closed

Eliminating Flicker Leaks

ByteTrack & Linear Velocity Interpolation

❌ Naive Frame-by-Frame Redaction

When an officer turns their head rapidly, motion blur causes detector confidence to momentarily drop from 0.85 to 0.20 for 4 consecutive frames.

Frame 40: Blurred [OK]
Frame 41: UNBLURRED LEAK! ⚠️
Frame 42: UNBLURRED LEAK! ⚠️
Frame 43: Blurred [OK]
Result: Instant privacy breach on video playback.
✅ ByteTrack + Velocity Coasting (Our Pipeline)

ByteTrack maintains track continuity across high and low-confidence detections. When a track is occluded or blurred out, our velocity interpolator coasts the bounding box along its trajectory.

Frame 40: Confirmed detection
Frame 41: Coasted (Velocity vector Δx, Δy) 🟡
Frame 42: Coasted (Velocity vector Δx, Δy) 🟡
Frame 43: Re-acquired & merged
Result: Seamless 100% anonymization across dropouts.
Coasting Limit: Up to 15 frames (~500ms) with forward-backward smoothing to prevent over-extension. max_coast = 15 frames
