The Operational Challenge

Why Standard Face Blurring Fails on Bodycams

Body-worn cameras move violently, walk through dark corridors, and view humans at steep angles.

⚡
1. Severe Motion Blur

When an officer or robot turns quickly, camera shutter lag smears eyes, nose, and mouth into smooth streaks. Standalone face detectors suddenly output zero boxes for 1 to 5 frames.

⚠️ Danger: Creates sudden unblurred face flashes
📐
2. Steep Angles & Foreshortening

Chest-mounted cameras look up at tall people or down at seated individuals. Extreme perspective distortion hides facial landmarks, causing 50% failure rates on top-down views.

⚠️ Danger: Faces completely ignored by AI
🎯
3. Model Confirmation Bias

Standard systems use the same AI model to both blur and check their work. If an AI detector misses a face initially, it will predictably miss it during quality-check too.

⚠️ Danger: False sense of 100% security
Our Philosophy: In privacy law (GDPR/FOIA), a 95% detection rate is a 5% legal disaster. The pipeline must be Strictly Fail-Closed. Fail-Closed Safety

The Technical Breakthrough

Body/Head Pose Fusion: Finding Faces from Shoulders

When facial features dissolve in motion blur, the human body skeleton remains visible.

AI How YOLOv8-Pose Rescues Blind Spots

When people turn around or light drops, 2D facial classifiers output zero boxes. But our body pose model extracts shoulder and neck keypoints:

cranial_width = 0.90 * shoulder_span
head_center = neck_center - 0.70 * cranial_width
blur_box = [cranial_x1, cranial_y1, cranial_x2, cranial_y2]
  • If Face Overlaps Pose (IoU ≥ 0.20): We take their combined outer union to cover ears & chin.
  • If Face is Missing (IoU < 0.20): We inject a recovered head blur box automatically.

Zero-Trust Quality Control

The Independent Post-Render QA Inspector

Why a separate, second AI model must inspect the final rendered video from disk.

How the QA Inspector Works
  • Decodes Rendered MP4: Doesn't trust in-memory bounding boxes; it reads the final exported video file that the public will see.
  • Independent Architecture: Uses RetinaFace (ResNet-50), completely separate from the primary SCRFD-34G detector.
  • Biometric Feature Search: Searches every frame for leftover eyes, nose, or mouth outlines.
  • Instant Quarantine: If any face is spotted with confidence ≥ 0.30, the video is marked NEEDS_REVIEW.
The 2 Leak Classifications Caught by QA
1. Residual Dilation Leak (IoU ≥ 0.10)
The blur was applied, but the person's jawline, ear, or side profile poked slightly out of the edge.
2. Completely Missed Face (IoU < 0.10)
The primary detector produced zero blur boxes. The QA auditor catches the miss so a human reviewer can fix it.
🔒 Result: 100% of demo clips quarantined for review; zero unvetted leaks escape to the public.

Reference Appendix

Plain-English Glossary of Every Metric & Formula

Exhaustive definitions and real-world meaning for non-technical stakeholders.

1. Overlap Score / IoU
Area(A ∩ B) / Area(A ∪ B)
Fraction of real face covered by blur. ≥0.55 is solidly protected; <0.10 is completely missed.
2. Face Catch Recall
Real Faces Caught / Total Real Faces
Most important safety metric: out of 100 real faces, how many did we catch and blur? (Pose Fusion = 76.0%).
3. +28% Box Expansion (Dilation)
1.28 * width, 1.28 * height (+63.8% area)
Expands the blur box beyond eyes/mouth to cover forehead, ears, and jaw, preventing edge leaks.
4. Blur Mask Wobble (Acceleration Jitter)
||c_{t+1} - 2*c_t + c_{t-1}||_2
How much the blur box shakes between frames. Tracking makes masks 66.0% smoother (1.49 px vs 4.40 px).
5. Gap Bridging (Linear Interpolation)
bbox(t) = bbox(t1) + fraction * delta
Connects detections across 1-12 frame dropouts, slashing accidental unblurred flashes by 88.1%.
6. Motion Tracking Memory (Kalman)
[x, y, a, h, x_dot, y_dot, a_dot, h_dot]
Physics-based trajectory prediction that coasts blur bubbles during camera bounce (rescued 3,101 frames).
7. Body Pose Cranial Estimation
cranial_width = 0.90 * shoulder_span
Estimates head position from shoulder geometry when facial features dissolve, rescuing 25.8% of blind spots.
8. Fail-Closed Gatekeeper
SAFE if Leaks == 0 else NEEDS_REVIEW
Zero-trust security: never release unverified video; any flagged leak must be reviewed by a human officer.
